TOAST SIMULATOR MD

A REAL MEGA DRIVE ROM, RUNNING IN AN EMULATOR

CONTROLS

  • Z X S or Enter: push the lever, then pop the toast.
  • On a phone, tap the big TOAST button.
  • Pop inside the yellow brackets for a PERFECT.

THE CARTRIDGE

This is toast.bin, a Mega Drive ROM written in C for the console's 68000. It runs in any Mega Drive emulator, or on a real console with a flash cartridge. The browser version is here.
