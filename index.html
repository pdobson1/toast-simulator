Push the lever and watch the needle. Pop it inside the yellow brackets for a PERFECT (10 TP), and keep it up for a streak bonus. Leave it too long and it burns. Every 6 rounds you go up a level: the toast cooks faster and the brackets get tighter. You get 3 lives a level, and anything less than a perfect costs one. From level 7, a miss also sends you back to 0/6. Clear level 10 to finish. Your score comes from perfects (harder breads score more), level clears, lives left and your best streak. TP and upgrades carry over from run to run. You can also tap the screen, or press Space.
